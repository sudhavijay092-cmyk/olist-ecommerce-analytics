# SwiftCart — Project Scope

## 1. Project Title

SwiftCart — Margin Under Pressure:
Quick-Commerce Profitability & Operations Intelligence

## 2. Company

SwiftCart is a fictional Indian quick-commerce company that operates
dark stores and delivers everyday products to customers.

This company is created solely for portfolio and educational purposes.

## 3. Business Context

SwiftCart has experienced growth in order volume and revenue.

However, management is concerned that contribution profit is not growing
at the same rate.

Management wants to understand which commercial and operational factors
are causing contribution margin leakage.

## 4. Core Business Problem

The company needs to identify where contribution margin is being lost
and determine which operational and commercial issues should be addressed
first.

Potential sources of margin leakage include:

- Excessive discounts
- High delivery costs
- Refunds
- Order cancellations
- Late deliveries
- Unprofitable customer segments
- Unprofitable product categories
- Weak dark-store operations
- Inefficient marketing campaigns

## 5. Main Business Question

Where is SwiftCart losing contribution margin, and which operational
and commercial actions should management take first?

## 6. Primary Stakeholder

COO / Operations Manager

## 7. Secondary Stakeholder

Commercial / Marketing Manager

## 8. Key Decisions the Analysis Should Support

The analysis should help management decide:

1. Which discount strategies should be reduced or redesigned?
2. Which delivery zones or operating conditions create excessive costs?
3. Which dark stores require operational improvement?
4. Which product categories generate weak contribution margins?
5. Which customer segments are profitable or unprofitable?
6. Which marketing campaigns generate economically attractive results?

## 11. Contribution Profit Concept

Contribution profit will be estimated as:

Net Revenue
- Product Cost
- Delivery Cost
- Payment Processing Cost
- Marketing Cost
- Refund Loss
- Customer Compensation
- Other defined variable operating costs

= Contribution Profit

Contribution Margin % will be calculated as:

Contribution Profit / Net Revenue

## 13. Data Approach

The project will combine:

1. Public anonymized e-commerce transaction data
2. Synthetic operational data generated using Python

The public data will provide the transactional foundation.

Synthetic data will provide operational variables that are not available
in the public source, such as delivery operations, dark-store information,
discount campaigns, refunds, inventory events and support issues.

## 14. Data Ethics

SwiftCart is fictional.

The project does not claim access to confidential information from any
real company.

Public data and synthetic data will be clearly separated.

Synthetic data will be generated using documented business rules and a
fixed random seed so that the process is reproducible.

No private personal data, login-protected data or illegally scraped data
will be used.

## 15. Project Limitations

The project will have several limitations:

- The public transactional data does not represent an actual Indian
  quick-commerce company.
- Synthetic operational data represents assumptions rather than actual
  company measurements.
- Product costs will be modeled rather than obtained from confidential
  company accounting systems.
- Delivery costs will be simulated using documented business rules.
- Marketing attribution will be simplified.
- Customer profitability will be an analytical estimate.
- Findings should not be presented as actual performance of SwiftCart or
  any real company.
