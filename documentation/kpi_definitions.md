# SwiftCart — KPI Definitions

## 1. Purpose

These KPIs are designed to measure SwiftCart's revenue quality,
operational efficiency, customer economics, and contribution profitability.

The KPIs will be calculated only after the underlying data has been
cleaned and validated.

---

# 2. Commercial KPIs

## 2.1 GMV

### Definition

Gross Merchandise Value represents the total selling value of products
included in orders before discounts and refunds.

### Formula

GMV = Sum of Order Item Selling Value

### Business Question

How much merchandise value is being generated?

---

## 2.2 Net Revenue

### Definition

Net Revenue represents revenue after applicable discounts and refunds.

### Formula

Net Revenue =
GMV
- Company-funded Discounts
- Refund Amount

### Business Question

How much revenue remains after major commercial leakage?

---

## 2.3 Average Order Value

### Definition

Average revenue generated per completed order.

### Formula

AOV = Net Revenue / Completed Orders

### Business Question

How much revenue does the average completed order generate?

---

# 3. Operational KPIs

## 3.1 Order Completion Rate

### Definition

Percentage of total orders that are successfully completed.

### Formula

Order Completion Rate =
Completed Orders / Total Orders

### Business Question

How reliably are orders being fulfilled?

---

## 3.2 Cancellation Rate

### Definition

Percentage of orders that are cancelled.

### Formula

Cancellation Rate =
Cancelled Orders / Total Orders

### Business Question

How much order volume is being lost before completion?

---

## 3.3 On-Time Delivery Rate

### Definition

Percentage of delivered orders completed within the promised delivery
time.

### Formula

On-Time Delivery Rate =
On-Time Delivered Orders / Delivered Orders

### Business Question

How reliably is SwiftCart meeting its delivery promise?

---

## 3.4 Average Delivery Cost per Order

### Definition

Average delivery-partner cost associated with each delivered order.

### Formula

Average Delivery Cost =
Total Delivery Cost / Delivered Orders

### Business Question

How expensive is fulfilment from a delivery perspective?

---

# 4. Customer KPIs

## 4.1 Repeat Customer Rate

### Definition

Percentage of active customers who completed more than one order during
the analysis period.

### Formula

Repeat Customer Rate =
Repeat Customers / Active Customers

### Business Question

How much of the customer base returns to SwiftCart?

---

## 4.2 Customer Contribution Profit

### Definition

Total contribution profit generated by a customer after applicable
variable costs.

### Formula

Customer Contribution Profit =
Customer Net Revenue
- Product Cost
- Delivery Cost
- Payment Processing Cost
- Marketing Cost
- Refund Loss
- Customer Compensation

### Business Question

Which customers create economic value rather than just revenue?

---

# 5. Product KPIs

## 5.1 Product Contribution Profit

### Definition

Contribution profit generated by a product or product category.

### Formula

Product Contribution Profit =
Product Net Revenue
- Product Cost
- Allocated Delivery Cost
- Allocated Discounts
- Refund Loss

### Business Question

Which products or categories are economically attractive?

---

## 5.2 Product Contribution Margin %

### Definition

Percentage of net revenue retained as contribution profit.

### Formula

Product Contribution Margin % =
Product Contribution Profit / Product Net Revenue

### Business Question

Which products generate strong margins?

---

# 6. Profitability KPIs

## 6.1 Contribution Profit

### Definition

Estimated profit remaining after variable commercial and operational
costs included in the project.

### Formula

Contribution Profit =
Net Revenue
- Product Cost
- Delivery Cost
- Payment Processing Cost
- Marketing Cost
- Refund Loss
- Customer Compensation

### Business Question

How much economic contribution does SwiftCart generate?

---

## 6.2 Contribution Margin %

### Definition

Contribution profit expressed as a percentage of net revenue.

### Formula

Contribution Margin % =
Contribution Profit / Net Revenue

### Business Question

How efficiently does SwiftCart convert revenue into contribution profit?

---

# 7. Customer Experience KPIs

## 7.1 Refund Rate

### Definition

Percentage of completed orders associated with a refund.

### Formula

Refund Rate =
Orders With Refunds / Completed Orders

### Business Question

How frequently are customers requiring financial remediation?

---

## 7.2 Average Review Score

### Definition

Average customer review score for orders where a review is available.

### Formula

Average Review Score =
Sum of Review Scores / Number of Reviewed Orders

### Business Question

Is customer experience improving or deteriorating?

---

## 7.3 Support Ticket Rate

### Definition

Percentage of orders associated with at least one customer-support
ticket.

### Formula

Support Ticket Rate =
Orders With Support Tickets / Total Orders

### Business Question

How frequently do orders create customer-service workload?

---

# 8. Margin Leakage Metrics

## 8.1 Discount Rate

### Definition

Company-funded discount as a percentage of GMV.

### Formula

Discount Rate =
Company-Funded Discount / GMV

### Business Question

How much selling value is being sacrificed through discounts?

---

## 8.2 Delivery Cost Rate

### Definition

Delivery cost as a percentage of net revenue.

### Formula

Delivery Cost Rate =
Delivery Cost / Net Revenue

### Business Question

How heavily is delivery economics affecting profitability?

---

## 8.3 Refund Rate by Value

### Definition

Refund value as a percentage of net revenue.

### Formula

Refund Value Rate =
Refund Amount / Net Revenue

### Business Question

How much revenue is being lost through refunds?

---

# 9. Primary Management KPI

## Contribution Margin %

Contribution Margin % is the primary management KPI for this project.

It should be investigated alongside:

- Revenue
- Order volume
- Discount rate
- Delivery cost rate
- Refund rate
- Cancellation rate
- On-time delivery rate
- Customer profitability
- Product profitability
- Marketing performance

A change in contribution margin should not be treated as a finding by
itself.

The analysis must identify the underlying drivers responsible for the
change.

---

# 10. KPI Governance Rules

1. KPI definitions must remain consistent across SQL, Python, Excel and
   Power BI.

2. No KPI should be calculated differently in different tools without a
   documented reason.

3. KPI calculations should use validated data.

4. Cancelled and completed orders must be handled according to documented
   business rules.

5. Refunds must not be double-counted.

6. Company-funded discounts must be distinguished from discounts funded by
   another party where applicable.

7. Synthetic cost assumptions must be documented.

8. Recommendations must not be made from KPI values alone. They must be
   supported by underlying analysis.

9. Final KPI values must come from the final validated dataset.

10. No financial finding should be invented before the data is generated
    and analysed.
